Made in Morbi.

We make our mugs in our own factory on the Maliya Highway in Morbi, Gujarat, one of India's ceramic centres. Clay preparation, forming, glazing, firing and packing happen under one roof, and we have been producing since 2022.

We supply homes, cafes, hotels, retailers, corporate buyers and exporters, in stock glazes or with your own colours and prints.

Certified to ISO 9001:2015, with CE and QSA certificates.
