Made in Morbi, since 2022.

A ceramic mug factory on the Maliya Highway in Gujarat. We make mugs for homes, cafes, hotels, retailers and brands, in stock glazes or your own colours and prints.

How a mug is made.

  1. 1

    Clay

    Clay is loaded into our plant, ground in a ball mill and extruded into billets, so every batch starts the same.

  2. 2

    Forming

    Mugs are formed on machine, then trimmed and finished by hand before they dry on racks.

  3. 3

    Glazing

    Each mug is dipped and sprayed in its glaze colour, inside and out.

  4. 4

    Firing

    Glazed mugs travel through a roller kiln that fires them at high temperature.

  5. 5

    Packing and dispatch

    Every mug is checked, packed in cartons and loaded for dispatch to your door or your port.
